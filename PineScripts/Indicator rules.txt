VOLUME, DELTA, AND REVERSAL RULES

All Buy, Sell, Total, and Delta values in this document are estimated from the
indicator's lower-timeframe candle aggregation. They are not exchange bid/ask
data. USDT values are the estimated volume multiplied by the chart close.

1. CORE VOLUME AND DELTA SERIES

- `totalVolumeUsdt` is the bar's traded USDT volume.
- `buyVolumeUsdt` and `sellVolumeUsdt` are the estimated directional USDT
  volumes.
- `deltaVol` is estimated Buy Volume minus estimated Sell Volume.
- `deltaPercent` is `(buyVolume - sellVolume) / (buyVolume + sellVolume) * 100`.
- `relativeVolume` (RVOL) is `barVolumeUsdt / SMA(barVolumeUsdt, whaleLength)`.
- The default `whaleLength` is 20 bars.

2. CONFIGURABLE VOLUME AND DELTA THRESHOLDS

Liquidity strength:
- `volumeLength`: 20 bars by default.
- `highVolumeMultiplier`: 1.0x by default.
- A confirmed pivot is marked high-volume when its pivot-bar USDT volume is
  greater than `SMA(barVolumeUsdt, volumeLength) * highVolumeMultiplier`.

Institutional-volume spike:
- `institutionalVolumeSmaLength`: 20 bars by default.
- `institutionalVolumeMultiplier`: 1.8x by default.
- An institutional volume spike requires
  `barVolumeUsdt > SMA(barVolumeUsdt, institutionalVolumeSmaLength) *
  institutionalVolumeMultiplier`.

Whale volume:
- `whaleLength`: 20 bars by default.
- `whaleMultiplier`: 2.0 by default.
- Whale Buy Volume requires
  `buyVolumeUsdt > SMA(buyVolumeUsdt, whaleLength) +
  whaleMultiplier * StDev(buyVolumeUsdt, whaleLength)`.
- Whale Sell Volume requires
  `sellVolumeUsdt > SMA(sellVolumeUsdt, whaleLength) +
  whaleMultiplier * StDev(sellVolumeUsdt, whaleLength)`.

Bull-sweep validation and momentum:
- `negativeDeltaThreshold`: -12.0% by default.
- `momentumDeltaThreshold`: 15.0% by default.
- `momentumCmfThreshold`: 0.05 by default.
- `requireBullConfirmation`: enabled by default.

OI anomaly thresholds:
- `oiSignificancePercent`: 15.0% of traded USDT volume by default.
- `neutralDeltaBound`: 15.0% by default.
- `bullishDeltaThreshold`: 15.0% by default.
- `bearishDeltaThreshold`: -12.0% by default.

3. REVERSAL PROBABILITY LABELS

3.1 Qualifying setups

Bullish sweep reversal:
- An SSL / sell-side liquidity sweep occurred during the current or previous
  three bars.
- Current total USDT volume is greater than 1.5x the whale volume average.
- Estimated Delta is positive (`deltaVol > 0`).

Bearish sweep reversal:
- A BSL / buy-side liquidity sweep occurred during the current or previous
  three bars.
- Current total USDT volume is greater than 1.5x the whale volume average.
- Estimated Delta is negative (`deltaVol < 0`).

Bullish exhaustion reversal, on 4-hour-or-higher charts only:
- Price overlaps an active bullish order block, or an SSL sweep occurred during
  the current or previous three bars.
- The lower wick is at least 55% of the full candle range.
- The candle closes green.
- Total USDT volume is below 85% of its 20-bar total-volume SMA.
- Estimated Delta is positive.

Bearish exhaustion reversal, on 4-hour-or-higher charts only:
- Price overlaps an active bearish order block, or a BSL sweep occurred during
  the current or previous three bars.
- The upper wick is at least 55% of the full candle range.
- The candle closes red.
- Total USDT volume is below 85% of its 20-bar total-volume SMA.
- Estimated Delta is negative.

Bullish order-block touch reversal, on every timeframe:
- The candle overlaps an active bullish order block.
- The candle closes green and above the prior close.
- The lower wick is at least 45% of the full candle range.
- The close is in the upper 40% of the candle range.
- Estimated Delta is positive.

Bearish order-block touch reversal, on every timeframe:
- The candle overlaps an active bearish order block.
- The candle closes red and below the prior close.
- The upper wick is at least 45% of the full candle range.
- The close is in the lower 40% of the candle range.
- Estimated Delta is negative.

An OB touch setup takes precedence over an exhaustion setup on the same candle.
An active order block can produce one touch label while price remains inside it;
it becomes eligible again only after price fully exits the block.

3.2 Final label rendering filter

Labels are drawn only when a qualifying setup starts, the bar is confirmed, and
these final checks pass:
- Bullish: the close is in the upper 40% of its range and above the prior close.
- Bearish: the candle is red, its close is in the lower 40% of its range, and
  it is below the prior close.
- Bullish labels are placed at `low - ATR(14) * 0.4`.
- Bearish labels are placed at `high + ATR(14) * 0.4`.

4. REVERSAL PROBABILITY SCORE

Probability is capped between 0% and 99%.

Base score:
- Sweep and exhaustion setups start with the historical completed-sweep success
  rate: `successful completed sweep targets / all liquidity sweeps * 100`.
- Order-block touch setups start at a fixed 40%.

Additional weights:
- Volume: +15 when current total USDT volume is greater than 2.0x its 20-bar
  total-volume SMA; otherwise +10 when greater than 1.5x; otherwise +0.
- Delta: +15 when `abs(deltaVol) / volume` is greater than 0.15; otherwise +10
  when greater than 0.08; otherwise +0.
- H4 corridor: +15 for a bullish qualifying setup at/below the lower corridor,
  or a bearish qualifying setup at/above the upper corridor. OB-touch setups
  do not receive this weight.
- CVD divergence: +15 when a recent confirmed CVD divergence agrees with the
  reversal direction. "Recent" means within `cvdDivergenceWindow`, five bars by
  default.
- Exhaustion setups receive no volume-weight bonus. Their base, Delta, H4, and
  CVD-divergence components remain applicable.

5. HIGH-VOLUME CLASSIFICATION

The High-vol threshold is asset-relative and recalculates on every bar:
- `dynamicVolumeSma = SMA(totalVolumeUsdt, 50)`.
- `highVolThreshold = dynamicVolumeSma * 1.5`.

`highVolThreshold` classifies a qualifying reversal using total USDT volume:
- STRONG: probability is 50% or higher.
- HIGH VOL: probability is below 50% and
  `totalVolumeUsdt >= highVolThreshold`.
- WEAK: probability is below 50% and
  `totalVolumeUsdt < highVolThreshold`.

6. REVERSAL LABEL CONTENT AND COLORS

- Bullish labels display `Buy: $...` using the estimated Buy USDT volume.
- Bearish labels display `Sell: $...` using the estimated Sell USDT volume.
- Values use the same compact K/M/B formatter as the dashboard.
- The strength classification always uses total USDT volume, not the displayed
  directional volume.
- Bullish STRONG labels are teal; bullish WEAK labels are orange; bullish HIGH
  VOL labels are blue.
- Bearish STRONG labels are orange; bearish WEAK labels are gray; bearish HIGH
  VOL labels are purple.

7. WHALE BUY AND WHALE SELL (BASE INDICATOR)

WHALE BUY requires all of the following:
- The candle overlaps an active bullish order block.
- Whale Buy Volume is true: Buy USDT volume exceeds its configured SMA plus
  `whaleMultiplier * standard deviation`.
- `deltaPercent > 0`.
- `totalVolumeUsdt > SMA(totalVolumeUsdt, 20)`.
- There is no active potential bearish reversal.

WHALE SELL requires all of the following:
- The candle overlaps an active bearish order block.
- Whale Sell Volume is true: Sell USDT volume exceeds its configured SMA plus
  `whaleMultiplier * standard deviation`.
- `deltaPercent < 0`.
- `totalVolumeUsdt > SMA(totalVolumeUsdt, 20)`.

If an active bearish order block remains above a Whale Buy, the base indicator
dashboard shows `WHALE BUY -> TGT: BEAR OB [price]`. The AI-copy override is
defined in Section 12.

8. VOLUME CLIMAX, ABSORPTION, AND MID-RANGE CONTEXT

Volume climax:
- `isHighVolume` and `isVolumeClimax` require
  `barVolumeUsdt > SMA(barVolumeUsdt, 20) * 1.8`.
- Low volume requires `barVolumeUsdt < SMA(barVolumeUsdt, 20) * 0.8`.

VOL CLIMAX ABS:
- An institutional volume spike occurs at an SSL or BSL sweep.
- Delta is positive.
- The prior candle is green.
- Current `deltaPercent` is below the prior candle's `deltaPercent`.
- Price is at the H4 upper corridor confluence.
- This condition plots the yellow `VOL CLIMAX ABS` marker and creates an orange
  absorption ceiling.

Dashboard absorption flags:
- `AbBuyV`: a red candle with positive Delta, CMF below 0.02, Buy ratio at or
  below 0.40, and a Volume Climax.
- `AbSellV`: a green candle with negative Delta, CMF above -0.02, Buy ratio at
  or above 0.60, and a Volume Climax.

Mid-range context:
- Mid-range volume requires `barVolumeUsdt > SMA(barVolumeUsdt, 20) * 1.25`.
- Price must be inside the locked H4 range and its statistical corridors.
- `MID BULL` requires this context and `deltaPercent > 20`.
- `MID BEAR` requires this context and `deltaPercent < -20`.
- CVD absorption context is also added when the corresponding three-bar hidden
  price/CVD divergence is present.

9. ORDER-BLOCK INVALIDATION AND OI ANOMALIES

Bullish order-block invalidation requires a close at/below the block bottom plus
one of:
- `deltaPercent < -25.0` and `relativeVolume > 1.5`; or
- Three-bar cumulative `deltaPercent < -40.0`.

Bearish order-block invalidation requires a close at/above the block top plus
one of:
- `deltaPercent > 25.0` and `relativeVolume > 1.5`; or
- Three-bar cumulative `deltaPercent > 40.0`.

OI significance requires:
- `abs(oiDeltaUsdt) > tradedVolumeUsdt * oiSignificancePercent / 100`.

When OI is significant:
- Institutional Accumulation: positive OI Delta and
  `abs(deltaPercent) <= neutralDeltaBound`.
- Aggressive Short Squeeze: negative OI Delta and
  `deltaPercent > bullishDeltaThreshold`.
- Aggressive Long Squeeze: negative OI Delta and
  `deltaPercent < bearishDeltaThreshold`.

10. CURRENTLY INACTIVE ABSORPTION INPUTS

`absorptionSmaLength` (default 10) and `absorptionVolumeMultiplier` (default
1.5) are exposed in settings but are not currently referenced by a signal,
filter, or trigger. Changing either setting does not change indicator behavior.

11. AI COPY: KNN CONFIDENCE SCORE

This section applies only to `Last Candle Volume Info_AI.pine`.

- The KNN classifier evaluates new bullish and bearish sweep-reversal events,
  not exhaustion-only or order-block-touch-only reversal setups.
- Each training sample contains:
  - `f1`: `deltaPercent`, clamped to the -100 to +100 range.
  - `f2`: the existing RVOL (`relativeVolume`) series.
  - `f3`: close position in the H4 corridor:
    `(close - lower_corridor) / (upper_corridor - lower_corridor)`, clamped to
    the 0 to 1 range.
- A sample is recorded only after its sweep-reversal event is confirmed and all
  three feature values are available.
- The outcome horizon is configurable through `KNN outcome horizon (bars)`,
  with a default of five bars. A bullish sample succeeds when its horizon close
  is above its entry close; a bearish sample succeeds when its horizon close is
  below its entry close.
- The AI memory is capped at 200 events. Each new sample and its pending or
  resolved outcome are pruned together with the oldest sample.
- Prediction compares the current features with resolved historical samples
  using Euclidean distance. Delta distance is divided by 100 so its clamped
  percentage scale is comparable to the other features.
- The five nearest resolved samples determine `aiConfidenceScore`:
  `successful neighbors / 5 * 100`.
- `aiConfidenceScore` remains unavailable until five resolved, feature-complete
  historical samples exist. When available, reversal labels append
  `| AI: NN%`; the score does not change existing reversal probability,
  strength, colors, targets, or alerts.

12. AI COPY: WHALE COUNTER-TREND SIGNAL OVERRIDE

This section applies only to `Last Candle Volume Info_AI.pine`.

- A WHALE BUY is rendered as `WHALE BUY 🐋`; it never appends a bearish
  order-block target.
- When a fresh WHALE BUY occurs against an active Bear target, C1 suppresses
  Bear-target text and displays `WHALE BUY 🐋`. Subsequent live recovery bars
  display `WHALE BUY | ACCUMULATION` until a newly confirmed bearish setup.
- The inverse rule applies to a fresh WHALE SELL against an active Bull target:
  C1 displays `WHALE SELL 🐋`, later recovery bars display
  `WHALE SELL | DISTRIBUTION`, and Bull-target text is suppressed until a newly
  confirmed bullish setup.
- The recovery override affects only C1. It does not rewrite C2-C10 historical
  dashboard rows or delete the active target line.

13. FIBONACCI TARGET RANGE CAP

This section applies to both `Last Candle Volume Info.pine` and
`Last Candle Volume Info_AI.pine`.

- The target range uses the latest 20 bars:
  - `swingHigh = highest high`
  - `swingLow = lowest low`
  - `swingRange = swingHigh - swingLow`
- Bearish sweep, reversal, and BOS/CHoCH order-block targets cannot be below
  `fibCapPrice = swingHigh - swingRange * 0.618`. A lower selected SSL or
  bullish order-block price is replaced with this cap.
- Bullish sweep and reversal targets cannot be above
  `fibCapPriceBull = swingLow + swingRange * 0.618`. A higher selected BSL
  price is replaced with this cap.
- The capped price is applied before target-distance validation and is reused
  for the yellow target line, target label, active target state, and dashboard
  target text.

14. AI COPY: LIVE TARGET DIRECTION SAFEGUARD

This section applies only to `Last Candle Volume Info_AI.pine`.

- After applying the Fibonacci cap, a bearish target at or above the current
  close is replaced with `low - ATR(14) * 0.5`.
- After applying the Fibonacci cap, a bullish target at or below the current
  close is replaced with `high + ATR(14) * 0.5`.
- The safeguard runs before target-distance validation and covers both selected
  liquidity targets and bearish BOS/CHoCH order-block continuation targets.
