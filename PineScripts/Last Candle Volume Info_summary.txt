AI LIQUIDITY MAP - CANONICAL INDICATOR REFERENCE

Scope
-----

This document is the canonical reference for `Last Candle Volume Info_AI2.pine`.
It consolidates the former graphic legend and the former indicator rules document.

All Buy, Sell, Total, Delta, and Open Interest values are estimates derived from
chart and lower-timeframe candle data. They are not exchange bid/ask data. USDT
values are estimated volume multiplied by the chart close.

1. LIQUIDITY MAP
----------------

- Confirmed pivot highs create Buy-Side Liquidity (BSL) levels. Confirmed pivot
  lows create Sell-Side Liquidity (SSL) levels.
- Nearby same-side pivots within `tolerancePercent` are merged as EQH/EQL by
  increasing touches rather than creating duplicate levels.
- Pivot strength is prioritized as EQH/EQL, then high-volume pivots, then normal
  pivots. High volume is determined from the pivot-bar USDT volume versus the
  configured volume SMA and multiplier.
- Active BSL levels are mint/teal and active SSL levels are crimson/pink. They
  extend right. The nearest active BSL above price and nearest active SSL below
  price are solid, width-3, fully opaque lines; every deeper active level stays
  width 1 with lower opacity.
- A level is mitigated by the selected `Wick Sweep` or `Close Breach` rule.
  Mitigated levels become gray dashed history when `keepMitigated` is enabled;
  otherwise their line is deleted.
- Stored liquidity zones are capped by `maxZones`; pruning removes the oldest
  zone and its line.

2. H4 LEVELS AND STATISTICAL CORRIDORS
--------------------------------------

- Locked H4 pivot high and low are the central H4 boundaries. On lower
  timeframes their optional projections are mint/pink dashed lines.
- The orange line above H4 High is the expected historical sweep deviation
  (`h4UpperCorridor`). The orange line below H4 Low is the corresponding lower
  deviation (`h4LowerCorridor`).
- The semi-transparent orange areas between H4 High/Low and these boundaries are
  statistical sweep corridors, not automatic entries.
- The top-right H4 panel shows distance from H4 High / Low, reversal probability,
  HTF trend and session context, recent CVD divergence and RVOL, and the
  distance to the active Bear and Bull squeeze levels.
- HTF trend is based on the configured higher-timeframe EMA and is context only:
  `AUKŠTYN` for up, `ŽEMYN` for down, and `DIAPAZONAS` for range.
- RVOL is `barVolumeUsdt / SMA(barVolumeUsdt, whaleLength)`. It is green at
  1.5x or higher and gray below 1.0x.
- Squeeze distance uses the close: `BEAR: +x%` measures the active fuchsia
  Short Squeeze line from above, while `BULL: -x%` measures the active cyan
  Long Squeeze line from below. Missing active lines display `NONE`.

3. TARGETS, INVALIDATION, AND REVERSALS
---------------------------------------

- An SSL sweep creates a Bull target at the best active BSL above price. A BSL
  sweep creates a Bear target at the best active SSL below price.
- Target selection uses zone priority first and distance second. It does not
  require BOS, CHoCH, or MSB.
- Only one yellow forecast target line can be active. A reached target becomes
  gray dashed history. A target cancelled, superseded, or expired is deleted.
- Bull targets are invalidated when price breaks below their origin sweep low.
  Bear targets are invalidated when price breaks above their origin sweep high.
  The originating dashboard row retains `REVERSAL / TGT CANCELLED`.
- An invalidated Bull target immediately scans for an SSL below price and flips
  to a Bear target; an invalidated Bear target performs the inverse scan.
- Target distance controls apply before rendering. Filtered targets show
  `TGT DISTANT` in the Signal / Bias column and do not draw a line.
- Targets are constrained to the latest 20-bar Fibonacci range:
  - Bear targets cannot be below `swingHigh - swingRange * 0.618`.
  - Bull targets cannot be above `swingLow + swingRange * 0.618`.
- After the Fibonacci cap, an invalid directional Bear target is placed at
  `low - ATR(14) * 0.5`; an invalid Bull target is placed at
  `high + ATR(14) * 0.5`. This runs before the distance filter.
- On Bear BOS or Bear CHoCH, the indicator may instead target the strongest
  active Bullish Order Block below price, selected by power, touches, then
  distance. The selected block's top is used directly, without a later
  Fibonacci cap or fallback adjustment, and both the target label and live C1
  dashboard cell read exactly `BEAR TARGET: OB`.
- A superseded origin row remains `SUPERCEDED -> BEARISH MOMENTUM` or
  `SUPERCEDED -> BULLISH MOMENTUM`; later bars do not recompute it.

4. ORDER BLOCKS AND WHALE SIGNALS
---------------------------------

- Confirmed pivot lows form the active Bullish Order Block stack (green);
  confirmed pivot highs form the active Bearish Order Block stack (violet).
  Invalidated blocks gray out and stop extending.
- Bullish block invalidation requires a confirmed close below its bottom and either:
  - `deltaPercent < -25` with `relativeVolume > 1.5`; or
  - three-bar cumulative Delta below -40%.
- Bearish block invalidation is the symmetrical confirmed close above top condition with
  `deltaPercent > 25` and RVOL above 1.5, or three-bar cumulative Delta above
  40%.
- A WHALE BUY requires overlap with an active Bullish Order Block, positive
  Delta, above-average total volume, high estimated Buy volume, and no active
  bearish reversal. A non-positive-Delta test shows `BULL OB TEST / AWAITING
  DELTA`.
- A WHALE SELL applies the symmetrical Bearish Order Block, negative-Delta,
  above-average volume, and high estimated Sell-volume requirements.
- While price remains below an active Bearish Order Block above it, only the live
  C1 dashboard row can be locked to `SELL-SIDE BIAS / BEAR OB CAP`. Historical
  C2-C10 rows are never rewritten.
- A confirmed Whale Buy below an uncleared Bearish Order Block is capped at
  `WHALE BUY -> TGT: BEAR OB [price]`.
- A fresh Whale Buy against an active Bear target temporarily displays
  `WHALE BUY`; following recovery bars show `WHALE BUY | ACCUMULATION` until a
  confirmed bearish setup. The inverse Whale Sell / Bull target behavior shows
  `WHALE SELL | DISTRIBUTION`. These live overrides do not delete a target line
  or rewrite history.

5. VOLUME, DELTA, CVD, AND OI
-----------------------------

- `totalVolumeUsdt` is traded volume times close. `buyVolumeUsdt` and
  `sellVolumeUsdt` are estimated directional USDT volume.
- `deltaVol` is estimated Buy volume minus Sell volume.
- `deltaPercent` is `(buyVolume - sellVolume) / (buyVolume + sellVolume) * 100`.
- Closed lower-timeframe candles are classified by candle direction. Pending
  live-bar volume is classified from incoming price movement. When intrabar data
  is unavailable, the indicator uses the parent candle's close position in its
  range. This estimate is never exchange bid/ask data.
- CVD is cumulative estimated Delta. Its slope relative to price is used for
  divergence, not its absolute value.
- At confirmed pivots, higher price high with lower CVD high is Bear CVD
  divergence; lower price low with higher CVD low is Bull CVD divergence.
  Chart triangles and the matching `BULL DIV` / `BEAR DIV` dashboard tag are
  plotted at the pivot bar.
- OI is significant when `abs(oiDeltaUsdt)` exceeds the configured percentage of
  traded USDT volume. Positive OI with neutral Delta marks accumulation; falling
  OI with strongly positive Delta marks a Short Squeeze; falling OI with strongly
  negative Delta marks a Long Squeeze.

6. REVERSAL QUALIFYING SETUPS
-----------------------------

Candle anatomy evaluation:
- `f_evaluateCandleAnatomy()` is evaluated once per bar and supplies the
  candle range, body, upper and lower wicks, their range-relative percentages,
  close position (0% at low, 100% at high), and green/red direction flags.
- A zero-range candle returns 0% for every anatomy percentage, preventing
  division by zero. The parent-candle fallback Buy/Sell estimate retains its
  neutral 50/50 split in that case.
- Exhaustion and Order-Block touch checks use the shared wick and close-position
  percentages. The AI2 score matrix receives the same anatomy values, including
  the Whale Distribution Wall test of a lower-half close and upper wick larger
  than the body.

Bullish sweep reversal:
- An SSL sweep occurred on the current or one of the preceding three bars.
- Current USDT volume exceeds 1.5 times the whale-volume average.
- Estimated `deltaVol` is positive.

Bearish sweep reversal:
- A BSL sweep occurred on the current or one of the preceding three bars.
- Current USDT volume exceeds 1.5 times the whale-volume average.
- Estimated `deltaVol` is negative.

Completed squeeze markers:
- A Short Squeeze Completed marker requires a BSL sweep in the last three bars,
  total USDT volume at least 1.15x its 50-bar SMA, an upper wick of at least
  35% of range, a close in the lower 55% of range, and either negative
  `deltaVol` or `deltaPercent` below 5%. It leaves a permanent neon-fuchsia
  square, a `size.tiny` three-line `⚠️ SSQ: PURGE` label with compact
  Buy/Sell USDT volume, fuchsia column glow, and a width-4 horizontal
  line from its high extending right. Only the most recent Short Squeeze line
  is active; a new confirmed Short Squeeze deletes the prior Short Squeeze
  line. It contributes a -30 directional score shift.
- A Long Squeeze Completed marker uses the symmetrical SSL sweep, high-volume,
  35% lower-wick, upper-55%-close, and either positive `deltaVol` or
  `deltaPercent` above -5% criteria. It leaves a permanent neon-cyan square,
  a `size.tiny` price-anchored `⏳ LSQ: PENDING (Bar 0/10)` label, cyan column
  glow, and a width-4 horizontal line from its low extending right. Only the
  most recent Long Squeeze line is active; a new confirmed Long Squeeze deletes
  the prior Long Squeeze line. It contributes a +30 directional score shift.

Bullish exhaustion reversal, available on 4H and higher:
- Price overlaps an active Bullish Order Block, or an SSL sweep occurred within
  three bars.
- The lower wick is at least 55% of the range, the candle closes green, total
  USDT volume is below 85% of its 20-bar SMA, and Delta is positive.

Bearish exhaustion reversal, available on 4H and higher:
- Price overlaps an active Bearish Order Block, or a BSL sweep occurred within
  three bars.
- The upper wick is at least 55% of the range, the candle closes red, total
  USDT volume is below 85% of its 20-bar SMA, and Delta is negative.

Order-Block touch reversal:
- A Bullish touch requires active Bullish OB overlap, a green close above the
  prior close, lower wick at least 45% of range, close in the upper 40% of range,
  and positive Delta.
- A Bearish touch requires active Bearish OB overlap, a red close below the
  prior close, upper wick at least 45% of range, close in the lower 40% of
  range, and negative Delta.
- An OB touch has precedence over exhaustion on the same candle. A block can
  emit one touch label until price completely exits the block. Its latch is
  reset only after a non-orange full exit; orange TEMA is context and does not
  suppress a qualifying OB-touch label.

Labels are drawn only when a qualifying setup starts on a confirmed bar. Bullish
labels require a close in the upper 40% of range and above the prior close;
Bearish labels require a red close in the lower 40% and below the prior close.
- Orange TEMA consolidation remains available as AI-score context but does not
  suppress qualifying OB-touch or exhaustion setups, their probability labels,
  or their label latches.

Nearest-liquidity and Long Squeeze status tracking:
- The nearest active BSL is the closest active pivot-high level strictly above
  the close. The nearest active SSL is the closest active pivot-low level
  strictly below the close. These same levels receive the emphasized liquidity
  styling described in the Liquidity Map section.
- `f_getMarketStructureSignal()` ranks each confirmed bar as Strong, Medium,
  Weak, or Neutral Buy/Sell using BOS/CHoCH, fresh or invalidated Order Blocks,
  Order Block tests, institutional volume, Delta, candle anatomy, and the
  institutional heavy-selling crash profile.
- `f_resolveSqueezeStatus()` consumes that structural result only during the
  first one to ten bars following a fresh Long Squeeze. A Buy Strong or Buy
  Medium result resolves the active line to `REAL_REVERSAL`; Sell Strong
  resolves it to `BREAKOUT_LIQUIDATION`; Sell Medium resolves it to
  `FAKE_REBOUND_TRAP`. A Sell Strong result or `heavySellingPressure` resolves
  liquidation immediately regardless of close position anywhere in the ten-bar
  window. A pending status reaching bar 11 becomes `TIMEOUT / EXPIRED`.
- The active squeeze label and live C1 Dashboard Signal / Bias cell directly
  read the resolved status, overriding lower-priority dashboard messages. A
  `REAL_REVERSAL` keeps the line cyan and solid with a teal label. Both
  `FAKE_REBOUND_TRAP` and liquidation draw a red dashed line; the former has
  an orange label, while liquidation stops the line and uses a transparent-red
  label. Pending labels show `⏳ LSQ: PENDING (Bar n/10)`, liquidation shows
  `❌ LSQ: BREAKOUT` plus the breakout candle's compact Buy/Sell USDT volumes
  on separate lines,
  a fake rebound shows `⚠️ LSQ: FAKE`, and reversal shows
  `🔥 LSQ: REVERSAL` plus its confirmation candle's compact Buy/Sell USDT
  volumes on separate lines. All active Long Squeeze label states use
  `size.tiny`. The active Long Squeeze label is price-anchored, and a breakout
  explicitly resets it to the Long Squeeze line's price. A timeout changes the
  line to thin gray dashed history and its label to `⌛ LSQ: EXPIRED`. No
  standalone inducement or fake-rebound labels are emitted.
- A Short Squeeze activation label is compact and includes the activation
  candle's estimated directional USDT volumes:
  `⚠️ SSQ: PURGE` followed by `B: $<buy>` and `S: $<sell>` on separate lines;
  it uses `size.tiny`.
- `f_formatDashboardSignal()` is the single source of text for the Dashboard
  Signal / Bias cell. It composes active context in order: squeeze status,
  selected structural label, market-structure verdict, then consolidation bias.
  Non-empty unique entries are joined with ` | `, so a cell can show
  `❌ LSQ: BREAKOUT | 🐂 BULL OB TEST / AWAITING DELTA` without duplicate text
  or trailing separators. The function accepts both internal squeeze state
  values and their compact display equivalents when determining the status.

7. REVERSAL PROBABILITY AND AI CONFLUENCE
-----------------------------------------

The final Reversal Probability Score is strictly bounded from 0% through 99%.

Base score:
- Sweep and exhaustion setups begin with the completed sweep-target success rate:
  `successfulSweepReversals / totalLiquiditySweeps * 100`.
  Before any tracked sweep result exists, they use a 45% baseline.
- Order-Block touch setups begin at 40%.

Standard weights:
- Volume: +15 above 2.0x the 20-bar total-volume SMA; otherwise +10 above 1.5x.
  Exhaustion setups receive no Volume weight.
- Delta: +15 when `abs(deltaVol) / volume > 0.15`; otherwise +10 when above
  0.08.
- CVD divergence: +15 when a confirmed direction-matching CVD divergence is
  within `cvdDivergenceWindow` (five bars by default).
  A Bullish qualifying setup with `BULL DIV` in that window and estimated Buy
  volume above $1,000,000 receives +25 instead. This heavy-buy confluence
  suppresses an opposing TEMA penalty.

AI confluence weights:
- SMT context: +15 for a Bullish qualifying sweep or OB-touch setup when
  `isAltStrong` is true (`ALT STRONG (BTC FALLING)`), or a Bearish qualifying
  sweep or OB-touch setup when `isAltWeak` is true (`ALT WEAK (BTC RISING)`).
  An otherwise qualifying setup facing the opposite SMT state receives -15.
- Absorption trap: +15 for a Bullish sweep reversal when `isBearBreakTrap` is
  true on this or the prior bar. The same +15 applies to a Bearish sweep
  reversal when `isBullAbs` is true on this or the prior bar.
- Dynamic H4 corridor depth: this replaces the legacy fixed +15 corridor boost
  and never applies to an OB-touch setup:
  - Bullish: `lowerBandWidth = h4Low - h4LowerCorridor`.
  - Bearish: `upperBandWidth = h4UpperCorridor - h4High`.
  - Shallow penetration: +10 when close lies in the first half of the relevant
    orange band beyond H4 Low / High.
  - Deep penetration: +20 when close reaches or exceeds the outer half of the
    relevant orange band.
- Vol Climax Abs resistance wall: +15 for a Bearish OB-touch reversal or BSL
  sweep when the setup high is within 0.05% of the most recent
  `volClimaxAbsCeiling`, provided that ceiling was established within 10 bars.
- TEMA trend confluence: +15 for a Bullish label when the smooth chart-timeframe
  TEMA state is lime, or a Bearish label when it is fuchsia.
- Live orange-TEMA consolidation bias feeds the directional score: a Bullish
  setup with `chopLean == 1`, or a Bearish setup with `chopLean == -1`, receives
  +10. A setup against the live lean receives -15. The final score remains
  clamped from 0% through 99%.
- Extreme institutional pressure is independent of the TEMA state: USDT volume
  above 1.8x its 20-bar SMA with Delta below -12% is heavy selling; Delta above
  +12% is heavy buying. Either contributes +30 and imposes a 75% minimum final
  score, capped at 99%. The top panel reports the matching momentum direction.
- When a reversal setup is active, AI2 compact-code confluence is folded into
  the UDF-derived score shift: `WW` or `WH` adds +30 and enforces the same 75%
  floor; `AH` adds +15; `AM` and `RM` add +5; `AL` and `RL` subtract 10.
  Other code combinations add no separate confluence.
- AI2 calculates a raw-volume `RVOL = volume / SMA(volume, whaleLength)` and
  applies the signed micro-variation `deltaPercent * 0.15 * RVOL` on every
  bar. This prevents static historical-ratio scores during consolidation while
  preserving the 0-99 clamp and extreme-pressure floor.

The confluence subtotal supplies the base change for the candle matrix. The
final aggregate advances sequentially on every bar:
`nz(aiScore[1], reversal baseline) + UDF-aligned score change + signed micro
volume Delta shift`, clamped to the 0-99 range. Thus an R1 top-rejection score
directly reduces `aiScore` rather than being bypassed by a detached weight.

When the clamped score reaches 85% or higher, `maximumAiProbability` is set
and the H4 panel uses `🔥 MAKSIMALI AI TIKIMYBĖ`. A confirmed bar at or above
that threshold also triggers the `Maximum AI reversal probability` alert.

8. HIGH-VOLUME CLASSIFICATION AND REVERSAL LABELS
-------------------------------------------------

- The dynamic High-vol threshold is recalculated every bar:
  `SMA(totalVolumeUsdt, 50) * 1.5`.
- `STRONG`: probability is at least 50%.
- `HIGH VOL`: probability is below 50% and total USDT volume meets or exceeds
  the High-vol threshold.
- `WEAK`: probability is below 50% and total USDT volume is below the threshold.
- Bullish labels are teal for STRONG, orange for WEAK, and blue for HIGH VOL.
  Bearish labels are orange for STRONG, gray for WEAK, and purple for HIGH VOL.
- Label text uses the same compact K/M/B formatter as the dashboard:
  `55% STRONG` plus `Buy: $...` for Bullish or `Sell: $...` for Bearish.
- Orange TEMA consolidation does not suppress reversal-probability labels or
  qualifying setups; heavy buying and selling pressure is explicitly eligible
  for the matching directional score. Order-Block-touch label latches remain
  paused during orange consolidation so a suppressed touch does not consume
  its future label opportunity. A fuchsia TEMA reduces the Bearish sweep volume
  gate from 1.5x to 1.125x (a 25% reduction).
- TEMA color never suppresses BOS, CHoCH, or primary sweep structures. A heavy
  selling Bear BOS/CHoCH receives a fallback structural label when the normal
  continuation path cannot draw one.
- The historical score stream is pure white and uses normal-size text. It
  shows the rounded change in reversal probability from the immediately prior
  bar: positive changes carry a `+` prefix, negative changes retain `-`, and
  unchanged scores display `0`.
- If the aggregate score is at least 85%, the H4 panel displays
  `🔥 MAKSIMALI AI TIKIMYBĖ` with the calculated percentage instead of
  `DIDELĖ TIKIMYBĖ`. Existing direction-relative colors are retained.

9. ABSORPTION, SMT, AND MID-RANGE CONTEXT
-----------------------------------------

VOL CLIMAX ABS:
- An institutional volume spike occurs at an SSL or BSL sweep with positive
  Delta.
- The prior candle is green and current `deltaPercent` is below the prior
  candle's `deltaPercent`.
- Price is at the H4 upper-corridor confluence.
- The yellow `VOL CLIMAX ABS` circle is plotted, an orange ceiling line is
  created, and the latest ceiling price and origin bar are retained for the
  resistance-wall score.

Absorption-trap markers:
- A Bear BOS or Bear CHoCH with estimated Buy volume more than 1.5x estimated
  Sell volume is a Bull Absorption Anomaly. Its label is prefixed with `⚠️`
  and a green `Bear Break Trap` square is plotted below the bar.
- A Bullish internal CHoCH with estimated Sell volume more than 1.5x estimated
  Buy volume is a Bear Absorption Anomaly. Its label is prefixed with `⚡`
  and a red `Bull Break Trap` square is plotted above the bar.

BTC SMT:
- Bearish SMT / ALT WEAK requires BTC rising over the configured lookback while
  the chart asset is failing. It appends `ALT WEAK (BTC RISING)`.
- Bullish SMT / ALT STRONG requires BTC falling while the chart asset holds. It
  appends `ALT STRONG (BTC FALLING)`.
- SMT is suppressed on BTC charts and when the chart symbol is the configured
  BTC reference asset, preventing self-comparison. SMT is intentionally
  dashboard-and-score only; it does not draw candle arrows or overlays.

Mid-range:
- Mid-range volume requires USDT volume above 1.25x its 20-bar SMA and a candle
  strictly inside the locked H4 range and statistical corridors.
- Delta above +20% adds `MID BULL`; Delta below -20% adds `MID BEAR`.
- Three-bar hidden price/CVD divergence adds `CVD ABSORP`.
- Mid-range and SMT messages append to existing Signal / Bias text with `|`;
  they do not replace a primary sweep, target, Whale, or Order Block message.

10. DASHBOARD AND GRAPHIC LEGEND
--------------------------------

The bottom-right dashboard has 10 fixed recent-candle rows and these columns:
`Bar`, `Buy`, `Sell`, `Total`, `Delta`, `CMF`, `OI Delta`, and `Signal / Bias`.

- Target-hit rows are blue. Cancelled-target origins are orange. Superseded target
  origins are dark charcoal.
- Signal / Bias owns sweep, target, target-hit, target-distance, failure,
  reversal, Whale, CVD-divergence, SMT, and context messages.
- Completed squeeze signals use compact dashboard codes: `SSQ` for a Short
  Squeeze and `LSQ` for a Long Squeeze. The code is colored fuchsia or cyan,
  respectively, and yields to higher-priority reversal, target-outcome, and
  live Bearish Order Block-cap messages.
- The dashboard is rendered only for the live last bar or last confirmed history.

Chart legend:
- Mint / teal line: active BSL.
- Crimson / pink line: active SSL.
- Orange lines and transparent fill: H4 statistical corridors.
- Yellow line: active Bull, Bear, or Bear OB forecast target.
- Gray dashed line: mitigated liquidity or reached target history.
- Green box: Bullish Order Block. Violet box: Bearish Order Block. Gray box:
  invalidated Order Block.
- Green triangle below a pivot: Bull CVD divergence. Red triangle above a pivot:
  Bear CVD divergence. Each includes the pivot candle's estimated directional
  volume label.
- Yellow circle above a bar: VOL CLIMAX ABS.
- Cyan diamond: institutional accumulation. Fuchsia `SQ` triangle: aggressive
  Short Squeeze. Orange `LQ` triangle: aggressive Long Squeeze. Fuchsia and
  cyan squares: completed Short and Long squeezes, respectively.
- 3-Color CM Institutional TEMA: lime for a rising trend, fuchsia for a
  falling trend, and orange during ATR-defined consolidation.

11. INSTITUTIONAL TEMA BIAS LINE
--------------------------------

- `CM TEMA Length` defaults to 20, `CM Color Smoothing Shift` defaults to 2,
  and `Consolidation ATR Sensitivity Multiplier` defaults to 0.15.
- The line uses the CM-style TEMA formula
  `3 * (EMA1 - EMA2) + EMA3` directly on the chart timeframe, so it remains
  smooth and aligned with the price candles.
- Orange is the default state and remains active whenever the absolute TEMA
  slope over the smoothing shift is below ATR(14) multiplied by the
  consolidation multiplier. Outside consolidation, it is lime when price is
  above the rising smooth TEMA and fuchsia when price is below the falling
  smooth TEMA.
- During orange consolidation, an internal lean uses responsive USDT volume and
  Delta thresholds: volume above 1.10x its 20-bar USDT SMA plus Delta above
  +12% is `ACCUMULATING BULL`; Delta below -12% is `DISTRIBUTING BEAR`;
  otherwise it is `PURE NEUTRAL`. The dashboard appends the applicable
  `BIAS: 🟡 CONSOLIDATION (...)` text in its Signal / Bias cell.
- In `Last Candle Volume Info_AI2.pine`, the unified
  `f_getSynchronizedScoreChange(isWhaleBuy, isWhaleSell, baseScore, vMult)`
  function calculates every historical and live score shift. `baseScore` is
  the change in the aggregate score before its AI2 micro-volume adjustment;
  `vMult` is RVOL times the configurable `volSensitivity` input.
- AI2 RVOL is `volume / SMA(volume, whaleLength)`, where `whaleLength`
  defaults to 20 and `volSensitivity` defaults to 1.0. Low-volume structural
  scores are correspondingly reduced, while Whale overrides use a minimum
  multiplier of 1.0.
- Score labels use a compact two-character matrix code: first character
  `W` for Whale override or distribution wall, `A` for absorption/rejection,
  or `R` for regular continuation/mute; second character is RVOL tier:
  `W` (>= 2.0), `H` (>= 1.5), `M` (>= 0.85), or `L` (< 0.85).
- Whale Buy and Whale Sell signals have absolute precedence after the
  distribution-wall check: both use the `W` family, with scores of
  `+15 * max(1.0, vMult)` and `-15 * max(1.0, vMult)`, respectively.
- The Whale Distribution Wall is also in the `W` family. It requires an H4
  upper-corridor touch or active Bearish OB overlap, Delta above +12%, a close
  in the lower half of the range, and an upper wick larger than the body. It
  produces `-20 * RVOL` before other Whale handling.
- Without a Whale signal, a Green candle with an upper wick greater than 1.2x
  its body and 40% of its range is in the `A` family, with `-10 * vMult`.
  Other Green candles are in the `R` family: a negative base score is muted
  to zero, otherwise the
  positive base score (or +5 baseline) is scaled by `vMult`.
- Without a Whale signal, a Red candle with a lower wick greater than 1.5x its
  body is in the `A` family, with `+10 * vMult`. Other Red candles are in the
  `R` family: positive base
  scores are inverted and scaled; zero uses `-5 * vMult`; existing negative
  scores are scaled negative.
- Every confirmed bar prints one normal-size white UDF score delta at the TEMA
  coordinate. The unclosed last bar uses one temporary `dynamicLiveAI` label.
  That label is deleted when the bar confirms, preventing a duplicate with the
  newly committed historical label.
- Each AI2 score label has two lines by default: its signed rounded score and
  the two-character matrix code. Once KNN has five resolved neighbors on an
  eligible new sweep-reversal event, an optional third line displays
  `AI: NN%`. No descriptive audit text or RVOL suffix is rendered.
- Specialized Bullish and Bearish probability labels use the primary
  probability format: calculated probability plus `STRONG` / `HIGH VOL` /
  `WEAK`, optional `AI: NN%` KNN confidence, then directional estimated Buy
  or Sell USDT volume. The generic score label remains responsible for the
  compact score/code format.

12. KNN CONFIDENCE SCORE (AI INDICATOR ONLY)
--------------------------------------------

- The KNN classifier evaluates new Bullish and Bearish sweep-reversal events,
  not exhaustion-only or OB-touch-only setups.
- Features are clamped Delta percent, current RVOL, and normalized close position
  inside the H4 corridor.
- A confirmed event stores its feature set, direction, entry close, and a pending
  outcome. After the configurable outcome horizon (five bars by default), a Bull
  sample succeeds above its entry close and a Bear sample succeeds below it.
- The memory retains at most 200 complete samples. Prediction uses Euclidean
  distance over the five nearest resolved samples.
- `aiConfidenceScore` is available only once five resolved feature-complete
  samples exist. When available, the generic compact score label appends
  `AI: NN%` after its code, while primary reversal labels use it as the
  middle line between probability/strength and directional volume.
- KNN confidence is informational: it does not alter Reversal Probability,
  classification or targets.
- When close is within 0.30% of an active squeeze line and raw volume is at
  least 1.25x its 20-bar SMA, the same resolved-sample KNN lookup runs as a
  proactive pre-sweep query. A confidence of at least 60% shows one live
  `HIGH RISK` SSQ/LSQ label at the appropriate active line and enables its
  matching alert; no synthetic fallback confidence is used.
