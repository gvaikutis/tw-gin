# Last Candle Volume Info - Graphic Items Reference

Source: PineScripts/Last Candle Volume Info.pine

Buy and sell values throughout this indicator are estimates. TradingView supplies total bar volume, so the script allocates it from the candle close's position within its high-low range. They are not exchange bid/ask figures.

## 1. Liquidity levels

### Local BSL and SSL lines
- A confirmed pivot high creates a BSL (buy-side liquidity) horizontal line in mint green.
- A confirmed pivot low creates an SSL (sell-side liquidity) horizontal line in crimson/red.
- Lines begin on the confirmed pivot bar and extend right while active.
- Normal levels are thin and dotted. High-volume levels are thicker and more visible. Equal highs/equal lows are solid, widest, and highest priority.
- A level is mitigated by either a wick sweep or close breach, according to the Mitigation input.
- Mitigated levels become gray dashed historical lines when "Keep mitigated levels" is enabled. Otherwise, they are deleted.
- The indicator stores up to the selected Maximum stored zones; the oldest level is removed first.

### H4 locked levels and statistical corridors
- `Locked H4 High`: mint-green, two-pixel stepline at the last confirmed higher-timeframe pivot high.
- `Locked H4 Low`: crimson/red, two-pixel stepline at the last confirmed higher-timeframe pivot low.
- `H4 High Statistical Corridor`: orange two-pixel stepline above the locked high.
- `H4 Low Statistical Corridor`: orange two-pixel stepline below the locked low.
- Each orange corridor is filled back to its associated locked H4 level with a very transparent orange area.
- On a chart below the selected H4 liquidity-source timeframe, optional dashed projections extend right:
  - H4 high projection: transparent mint-green dashed line.
  - H4 low projection: transparent crimson/red dashed line.

## 2. Order-block rectangles and their labels

### Bullish order blocks
- A confirmed pivot low creates a green bullish order-block rectangle from that pivot candle's high to low.
- The green rectangle starts at the pivot bar and expands right while active.
- Bullish order-block rectangles do not display a Buy-volume label.
- Several active bullish blocks may be present. An invalidated block stops extending and turns gray.

### Bearish order blocks
- A confirmed pivot high creates a purple/violet bearish order-block rectangle from that pivot candle's high to low.
- The rectangle starts at the pivot bar and expands right while active.
- A small dark-red `Bear OB` label is anchored at the rectangle's upper edge and follows its right side.
- Its text shows the accumulated estimated sell volume and delta percentage for merged nearby blocks.
- Several active bearish blocks may be present. An invalidated block stops extending, turns gray, and its label is removed.

### Orange exhaustion supply zone
- An orange, very transparent rectangle can appear after an exhaustion-reversal condition.
- It spans the exhaustion candle high to low and extends right.
- It stops extending when invalidated or when its short tracking window finishes.
- The dashboard reports the associated state, such as `AWAITING MOMENTUM`, `EXH CONFIRMED`, `DISTRIB. ZONE`, or `EXH INVALIDATED`.

## 3. Targets and structural labels

### Forecast target line and target tag
- Only one active target is shown at a time.
- A bullish target is a yellow horizontal line above price with a yellow `BULL TARGET` tag.
- A bearish target is a yellow horizontal line below price with a yellow `BEAR TARGET` tag.
- A bearish structure-continuation target that points to a bullish order block is labeled `BEAR TARGET: OB`.
- Target lines extend right. Reached targets become gray dashed historical lines; cancelled, superseded, distant, or expired targets are removed.
- The dashboard Signal / Bias cell records target outcomes such as target hit, cancellation, reversal, or distance filtering.

### Bearish BOS / CHoCH event box
- A small crimson/red label is placed above the relevant candle for a bearish BOS or CHoCH continuation event.
- Its title is `Bear BOS`, `Bear CHoCH`, or the same title with `+ LTF Bear CHoCH`.
- It now shows two volume lines: estimated `Buy: $...` and `Sell: $...`.
- A `⚠️ ` prefix marks a Bull Absorption Anomaly: estimated buy volume is more than 1.5x estimated sell volume during the bearish break. It warns that aggressive buying may be absorbed and the break can be a bearish fakeout.
- The same label-emission branch sets a bright green square below that exact bearish-break candle (`Bear Break Trap (Bull Abs)`).
- The label is offset upward when needed to reduce collisions with reversal labels.

### LTF CHoCH event boxes
- A mint-green `LTF Bull CHoCH` label is placed below a bullish internal change-of-character candle.
- A crimson/red `LTF Bear CHoCH` label is placed above a bearish internal change-of-character candle.
- Both labels show estimated `Buy: $...` and `Sell: $...`.
- A `⚡ ` prefix marks a Bear Absorption Anomaly on a bullish break when estimated sell volume is more than 1.5x estimated buy volume. A `⚠️ ` prefix marks the inverse Bull Absorption Anomaly on a bearish break.
- The same label-emission branch sets a bright red square above the exact bullish-break candle when a Bear Absorption Anomaly is present (`Bull Break Trap (Bear Abs)`).
- They are controlled by the `Show CHoCH labels` input.

## 4. Reversal-probability labels

### Bullish reversal label
- A label appears below price when a new bullish reversal setup is confirmed.
- It contains probability, classification, and directional volume; for example:
  `55% STRONG`
  `Buy: $140.76M`
- Colors:
  - Orange: weak reversal.
  - Blue: low-probability but high-volume reversal.
  - Teal: strong reversal.
- Only the latest bullish label is retained; a new bullish label replaces the previous one.

### Bearish reversal label
- A label appears above price when a new bearish reversal setup is confirmed.
- It contains probability, classification, and directional volume; for example:
  `43% HIGH VOL`
  `Sell: $229.53M`
- Colors:
  - Gray: weak reversal.
  - Purple: low-probability but high-volume reversal.
  - Orange: strong reversal.
- Only the latest bearish label is retained; a new bearish label replaces the previous one.

The probability combines the script's observed sweep-reversal rate with volume, delta, H4 corridor confluence, and agreeing CVD divergence.

## 5. Candle markers, OI annotations, and absorption levels

### Volume and reversal markers
- Yellow circle above candle: `VOL CLIMAX ABS`, showing volume climax/absorption at the H4 high context.
- Small semi-transparent green circle below candle: `MID BULL`. It requires the Mid-Range Context module, USDT candle volume above 1.25x the USDT volume SMA, price strictly inside the locked H4 levels and their statistical corridors, and Delta above +20%.
- Small semi-transparent red circle above candle: `MID BEAR`. It uses the same Mid-Range Context volume and H4-range requirements, with Delta below -20%.
- Small semi-transparent orange downward triangle below candle: bullish hidden CVD absorption. It requires the same Mid-Range Context conditions, a current low above the low three bars ago, and current CVD below CVD three bars ago.
- Small semi-transparent orange upward triangle above candle: bearish hidden CVD absorption. It requires the same Mid-Range Context conditions, a current high below the high three bars ago, and current CVD above CVD three bars ago.
- Orange downward triangle above candle with `EXH`: an exhaustion-reversal event.
- Mint-green tiny upward triangle below the pivot bar: bullish CVD divergence. A dark-green `Buy: $...` label directly below it shows that pivot candle's estimated buy value.
- Crimson/red tiny downward triangle above the pivot bar: bearish CVD divergence. A dark-red `Sell: $...` label directly above it shows that pivot candle's estimated sell value.
- Aqua diamond below candle: institutional accumulation, requiring significant positive open-interest change with neutral delta.
- Fuchsia upward triangle below candle with `SQ`: aggressive short squeeze, requiring significant negative OI change with strongly positive delta.
- Orange downward triangle above candle with `LQ`: aggressive long squeeze, requiring significant negative OI change with strongly negative delta.

### Open-interest value annotations
- Aqua plain text below an institutional-accumulation candle: positive OI change in USDT, such as `+$1.25M`.
- Fuchsia plain text below a short-squeeze candle: negative OI change in USDT.
- Orange plain text above a long-squeeze candle: negative OI change in USDT.

### Absorption ceiling
- An absorption event creates a solid orange horizontal resistance line at that candle's high.
- It extends right while active.
- When price closes above it, it stops extending and becomes thin gray dashed history.

### Invalidated order-block diagnostics
- Gray X below a candle: a bullish order block was invalidated.
- Gray X above a candle: a bearish order block was invalidated.
- When `Log invalidated Order Blocks` is enabled, the same invalidation prints a blue diagnostic label:
  - Below price for a bullish-block failure.
  - Above price for a bearish-block failure.
  - The label lists estimated buy/sell volume, Delta, CVD, and RVOL.

## 6. Tables

### Top-right H4 analysis panel
- A two-column, five-row panel headed `H4 Lygių Analizė` and `Live`.
- Rows:
  1. `Atstumas nuo H4 High / Low`: percentage distance from both locked H4 levels.
  2. `Apsisukimo tikimybė:`: current or latest reversal probability and direction.
  3. `Trendas (HTF)`: higher-timeframe EMA context: `AUKŠTYN` (up), `ŽEMYN` (down), or `DIAPAZONAS` (range).
  4. `CVD / RVOL (x vidurkis)`: most recent CVD divergence state and relative volume multiple.
- The reversal-probability cell changes background by confidence; the trend and divergence text use their directional colors.

### Bottom-right 10-candle dashboard
- An eight-column table covering the current candle (`C1`) and nine prior candles (`C2` through `C10`).
- Columns:
  1. `Bar`
  2. `BUY (USDT)` - estimated buy volume
  3. `SELL (USDT)` - estimated sell volume
  4. `TOTAL (USDT)` - total traded value
  5. `DELTA` - estimated buy/sell imbalance percentage
  6. `CMF` - Chaikin Money Flow
  7. `OI DELTA` - OI change and total OI in USDT
  8. `SIGNAL / BIAS` - liquidity, target, order-block, whale, divergence, and reversal state
- Buy figures use mint-green text; sell figures use crimson/red text. Positive/negative Delta and CMF use the same directional colors.
- Signal backgrounds provide visual status:
  - Teal/orange for active reversal and order-block testing context.
  - Blue for reached targets.
  - Orange for cancelled targets.
  - Dark charcoal for superseded targets.
  - Green/red tints for bullish/bearish signals.
- The bearish order-block cap can lock only live C1 to `SELL-SIDE BIAS / BEAR OB CAP`; historical C2-C10 rows are not overwritten.
- Mid-Range Context is append-only. `MID BULL (+...%)`, `MID BEAR (-...%)`, and/or `CVD ABSORP` are appended with ` | ` to existing primary messages rather than replacing them. When no primary message exists, the context is shown on its own.

### Bottom-left optional failure table
- Appears only when `Show invalidated Order Block failure table` is enabled.
- Shows the five most recent failures with four columns: `Time`, `Type`, `Close Price`, and `Volume`.
- A row type is `Bull OB Fail` or `Bear OB Fail`.

## 7. Data Window only - not chart graphics

When `Expose per-candle metrics in Data Window (CSV export)` is enabled, the following hidden plots are available for Data Window inspection or CSV export but are not drawn on the chart:
- Reversal Probability %
- Delta %
- CMF
- CVD
- RVOL
- Buy Volume (USDT)
- Sell Volume (USDT)
- Sweep Penetration %
- Reversal Flag (1 bullish / -1 bearish)
- CVD Divergence Flag
- HTF Trend (1 up / -1 down)
