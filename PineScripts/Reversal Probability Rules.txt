REVERSAL PROBABILITY LABEL RULES

1. WHEN A PROBABILITY LABEL IS DRAWN

Bullish probability label (below the candle):
- An SSL / sell-side liquidity sweep occurred during the current or previous 3 bars.
- Current total volume is greater than 1.5 x the whale volume average.
- Delta is positive: estimated Buy Volume is greater than estimated Sell Volume.

Bearish probability label (above the candle):
- A BSL / buy-side liquidity sweep occurred during the current or previous 3 bars.
- Current total volume is greater than 1.5 x the whale volume average.
- Delta is negative: estimated Sell Volume is greater than estimated Buy Volume.

2. PROBABILITY SCORE

Probability = historical sweep reversal success rate + volume weight + Delta weight + H4 confluence weight.
The result is limited to the 0% to 99% range.

Historical sweep reversal success rate:
- successful completed sweep targets / all detected liquidity sweeps x 100.

Volume weight:
- +15 when current total volume is greater than 2.0 x its 20-bar total-volume SMA.
- +10 when current total volume is greater than 1.5 x its 20-bar total-volume SMA.
- +0 otherwise.

Delta weight:
- +15 when abs(Delta) / total volume is greater than 0.35.
- +10 when abs(Delta) / total volume is greater than 0.20.
- +0 otherwise.

H4 confluence weight:
- +15 for a bullish reversal setup that reaches or moves below the lower H4 statistical corridor.
- +15 for a bearish reversal setup that reaches or moves above the upper H4 statistical corridor.
- +0 when neither condition applies.

3. STRENGTH CLASSIFICATION

- STRONG: probability is 50% or higher.
- WEAK: probability is below 50% and total volume is below the High-volume reversal threshold.
- HIGH VOL: probability is below 50% and total volume is at or above the High-volume reversal threshold.

The default High-volume reversal threshold is 10,000,000. The `highVolThreshold` integer input can be changed in the indicator settings.

4. PROBABILITY LABEL COLORS

ORANGE
- Bullish orange: a bullish reversal label with probability below 50% and total volume below the High-volume reversal threshold. Text: WEAK. The label is below price and shows Buy: X.XXK/M.
- Bearish orange: a bearish reversal label with probability of 50% or higher. Text: STRONG. The label is above price and shows Sell: X.XXK/M.

BLUE
- Bullish blue: a bullish reversal label with probability below 50% and total volume at or above the High-volume reversal threshold. Text: HIGH VOL. The label is below price and shows Buy: X.XXK/M.

PURPLE
- Bearish purple: a bearish reversal label with probability below 50% and total volume at or above the High-volume reversal threshold. Text: HIGH VOL. The label is above price and shows Sell: X.XXK/M.

OTHER COLORS
- Teal: bullish STRONG label; probability is 50% or higher.
- Gray: bearish WEAK label; probability is below 50% and total volume is below the High-volume reversal threshold.

5. VOLUME DISPLAY

- Bullish probability labels display estimated Buy Volume, formatted with the same K/M formatter as the dashboard.
- Bearish probability labels display estimated Sell Volume, formatted with the same K/M formatter as the dashboard.
- The HIGH VOL classification always uses total volume, not the displayed Buy or Sell volume.
- Probability labels are created only when the candle is confirmed, so their displayed directional volume is the same finalized dashboard series value for that bar.
- Displayed Buy and Sell label volume is bounded to the current bar's total volume, preventing an invalid negative or above-total estimate.

6. EXHAUSTION REVERSAL SETUP

This alternative probability-label trigger runs only on 4-hour-or-higher charts: 240-minute and higher intraday charts, Daily, Weekly, or Monthly.

Bullish exhaustion label (below price):
- Price touches an active bullish order block or an SSL sweep occurred during the current or previous 3 bars.
- The lower wick is at least 55% of the full candle range and the candle closes green.
- Total volume is below 85% of its 20-bar total-volume SMA.
- Delta is positive.

Bearish exhaustion label (above price):
- Price touches an active bearish order block or a BSL sweep occurred during the current or previous 3 bars.
- The upper wick is at least 55% of the full candle range and the candle closes red.
- Total volume is below 85% of its 20-bar total-volume SMA.
- Delta is negative.

Exhaustion labels use the same explicit probability-label renderer as every other setup: XX% BULL REVERSAL or XX% BEAR REVERSAL on the first line, then Buy or Sell volume on the second line. Their arrow indicates direction. They receive no volume-weight bonus; historical, Delta, and H4 confluence weights remain active. After an exhaustion label, all exhaustion labels are blocked for the next 5 candles.

7. OB TOUCH REVERSAL SETUP

This independent probability-label trigger does not require an SSL or BSL sweep and runs on every chart timeframe.

Bullish OB touch label (below price):
- The current candle overlaps an active bullish order block.
- The candle closes green.
- The lower wick is at least 45% of the full candle range.
- The close is in the upper 40% of its range and is above the prior candle close.
- Delta is positive.

Bearish OB touch label (above price):
- The current candle overlaps an active bearish order block.
- The candle closes red.
- The upper wick is at least 45% of the full candle range.
- The close is in the lower 40% of its range and is below the prior candle close.
- Delta is negative.

OB touch labels use the same explicit probability-label renderer as every other setup: XX% BULL REVERSAL or XX% BEAR REVERSAL on the first line, then Buy or Sell volume on the second line. Bullish OB labels are teal with white text; bearish OB labels are orange with white text. Their probability starts from a fixed 40% base and adds the regular Volume and Delta weights. H4 confluence is not added to this no-sweep setup. When an OB touch and exhaustion setup qualify on the same candle, the OB touch label takes precedence. Each active OB can produce only one OB touch label while price remains within it; its label eligibility resets only after price fully exits that block.

8. COMMON REVERSAL LABEL SAFETY FILTER

Every reversal label, including sweep, exhaustion, and OB touch labels, must pass these final rendering checks:
- Bullish labels require a close in the upper 40% of the candle range and a close above the prior candle close.
- Bearish labels require a red candle, a close in the lower 40% of the candle range, and a close below the prior candle close.
- Reversal labels use exact price coordinates: bullish labels are placed at low - ATR(14) x 0.4 and bearish labels at high + ATR(14) x 0.4. LTF CHoCH labels use the bar-relative belowbar or abovebar location.
- Labels format `dashboardBuyVolume[0]` or `dashboardSellVolume[0]`, respectively, so they always display the local C1 dashboard series value, never an Order Block's accumulated volume.
