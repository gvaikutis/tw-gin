REVERSAL PROBABILITY LABEL RULES

1. WHEN A PROBABILITY LABEL IS DRAWN

Bullish probability label (below the candle):
- An SSL / sell-side liquidity sweep occurred during the current or previous 3 bars.
- Current total volume is greater than 1.5 x the whale volume average.
- Delta is positive: estimated Buy Volume is greater than estimated Sell Volume.

Bearish probability label (above the candle):
- A BSL / buy-side liquidity sweep occurred during the current or previous 3 bars.
- Current total volume is greater than 1.5 x the whale volume average.
- Delta is negative: estimated Sell Volume is greater than estimated Buy Volume.

2. PROBABILITY SCORE

Probability = historical sweep reversal success rate + volume weight + Delta weight + H4 confluence weight.
The result is limited to the 0% to 99% range.

Historical sweep reversal success rate:
- successful completed sweep targets / all detected liquidity sweeps x 100.

Volume weight:
- +15 when current total volume is greater than 2.0 x its 20-bar total-volume SMA.
- +10 when current total volume is greater than 1.5 x its 20-bar total-volume SMA.
- +0 otherwise.

Delta weight:
- +15 when abs(Delta) / total volume is greater than 0.35.
- +10 when abs(Delta) / total volume is greater than 0.20.
- +0 otherwise.

H4 confluence weight:
- +15 for a bullish reversal setup that reaches or moves below the lower H4 statistical corridor.
- +15 for a bearish reversal setup that reaches or moves above the upper H4 statistical corridor.
- +0 when neither condition applies.

3. STRENGTH CLASSIFICATION

- STRONG: probability is 50% or higher.
- WEAK: probability is below 50% and total volume is below the High-volume reversal threshold.
- HIGH VOL: probability is below 50% and total volume is at or above the High-volume reversal threshold.

The default High-volume reversal threshold is 10,000,000. It can be changed in the indicator settings.

4. PROBABILITY LABEL COLORS

ORANGE
- Bullish orange: a bullish reversal label with probability below 50% and total volume below the High-volume reversal threshold. Text: WEAK. The label is below price and shows Buy: X.XXK/M.
- Bearish orange: a bearish reversal label with probability of 50% or higher. Text: STRONG. The label is above price and shows Sell: X.XXK/M.

BLUE
- Bullish blue: a bullish reversal label with probability below 50% and total volume at or above the High-volume reversal threshold. Text: HIGH VOL. The label is below price and shows Buy: X.XXK/M.

PURPLE
- Bearish purple: a bearish reversal label with probability below 50% and total volume at or above the High-volume reversal threshold. Text: HIGH VOL. The label is above price and shows Sell: X.XXK/M.

OTHER COLORS
- Teal: bullish STRONG label; probability is 50% or higher.
- Gray: bearish WEAK label; probability is below 50% and total volume is below the High-volume reversal threshold.

5. VOLUME DISPLAY

- Bullish probability labels display estimated Buy Volume, formatted with the same K/M formatter as the dashboard.
- Bearish probability labels display estimated Sell Volume, formatted with the same K/M formatter as the dashboard.
- The HIGH VOL classification always uses total volume, not the displayed Buy or Sell volume.
